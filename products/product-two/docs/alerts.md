# Alerts

Product Two watches your websites and APIs and tells you when they go down.

## Alert channels

Product Two can notify you by email, SMS and Slack. SMS alerts are available on the Business plan only.

## Alert thresholds

A check is considered down after 3 consecutive failures. The default check interval is 60 seconds. The fastest interval, 10 seconds, is available on the Business plan.

## Maintenance windows

Schedule a maintenance window to pause alerts. Alerts resume automatically when the window ends.
