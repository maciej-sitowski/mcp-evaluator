# Pricing

## Plans

Starter: $10 per month, 10 checks, 5-minute interval. Business: $49 per month, 100 checks, 10-second interval and SMS alerts.

## Billing

Plans are billed monthly. Cancel any time from Settings → Billing. There is no yearly discount.

## Free trial

Every account gets a 14-day free trial of the Business plan, no credit card required.
