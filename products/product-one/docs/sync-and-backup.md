# Sync and backup

## How sync works

Notes sync across all your devices within 10 seconds when you are online. Edits made offline sync as soon as you reconnect.

## Backups

Product One keeps daily backups for 30 days. Admins can restore a deleted note from the Trash within 30 days.

## Export your notes

Export to Markdown or PDF from Settings → Export. Exports of more than 1,000 notes are emailed as a ZIP file.
