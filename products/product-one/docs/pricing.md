# Pricing

## Plans

Free: up to 100 notes and 1 workspace. Pro: $8 per user per month with unlimited notes. Team: $15 per user per month, adds SSO and an audit log.

## Billing

Plans are billed monthly or yearly. Yearly billing saves 20%. Cancel any time from Settings → Billing.

## Student discount

Students get Pro free for one year with a valid .edu email address.
